Job Market Analysis

Project Overview -
This project focuses on analyzing job market data to understand employment trends, salary distributions and the skills required for different job opportunities.
The objective is to explore job-related information and identify the most in-demand technical skills in the job market.

Dataset-
Total Records: 742,
Total Features: 42

Tools & Technologies:
SQL – Data querying, cleaning and analysis,
Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation

Project Objectives- 
Analyze salary information across job opportunities.
Explore job locations and their distribution.
Identify the most frequently required technical skills.
Understand the demand for different programming languages and tools.
Explore trends in data-related job requirements.

Key Analysis- 
Salary distribution analysis.
Job location analysis.
Analysis of in-demand skills.
Comparison of technical skills such as Python, SQL, Excel, AWS, Spark, Tableau and Power BI.
Exploration of job market requirements.

Conclusion:
This project provides an overview of job market requirements, salary information and technical skills in demand. The analysis helps understand the skills and technologies that employers look for when hiring candidates.
