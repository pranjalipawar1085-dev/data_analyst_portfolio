Taco Bell Fast Food Analysis

Project Overview:
This project focuses on analyzing fast-food nutritional data, with a specific focus on Taco Bell. The objective is to examine the nutritional content of food items and identify insights related to calories, protein, fats, carbohydrates, sugar and sodium.

Dataset:
Total Records: 515,
Selected Restaurant: Taco Bell,
Total Taco Bell Items: 113

Tools & Technologies:
SQL – Data querying and analysis,
Microsoft Excel – Data analysis and calculations,
Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation

Project Objectives:
1.Analyze the nutritional information of Taco Bell food items.
2.Identify high-calorie and high-sodium food items.
3.Analyze protein, fat, carbohydrate and sugar content.
4.Compare nutritional averages across restaurants.
5.Identify nutritional patterns and insights.

Key Analysis:
Top 10 highest-calorie food items.
Protein and sodium analysis.
Average nutritional values across restaurants.
Comparison of nutritional components.
Identification of healthier and less healthy food options based on nutritional values.

Conclusion:
This analysis provides insights into the nutritional composition of Taco Bell menu items and helps understand how different fast-food products compare in terms of nutritional content.
